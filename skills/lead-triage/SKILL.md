---
name: lead-triage
description: Policy for first-pass triage of inbound marketing-agency leads into QUALIFY, NURTURE, REJECT or ESCALATE.
---

Decisions (choose exactly one):
- QUALIFY: clear marketing need, credible company, and monthly budget of about USD 5,000 or more.
- NURTURE: real interest but small or unclear budget, a later timeline, missing discovery info, an agency researching services for a client, or a company-size and budget mismatch (likely a typo) when the other details are consistent.
- REJECT: not a buyer (student, job seeker, vendor spam), junk, phishing or malware pretexts, privacy or data-erasure requests (these are not leads; they are routed to the privacy team), or budget under about USD 1,000 with no strategic signal.
- ESCALATE: a human must decide: conflicting signals alongside other concerns, unverifiable claims, or a costly wrong call.

ESCALATE needs a concrete risk (conflicting data, a claim that cannot be verified, legal or privacy content). Uncertainty about intent is not one. A missing message, or an agency or consultant asking about services on behalf of a client, is NURTURE when the company, website, email domain and budget are consistent.

Web verification (field "web_verification"):
- These are facts from a Google search run by a separate checker: how many results came back, whether the lead's website domain appears in them, whether the company name appears in result titles, and whether a knowledge panel exists.
- They are weak hints. No results is NOT evidence of fraud (many real small companies have a thin web presence), and results do NOT prove the person who submitted the form works there.
- Never QUALIFY because of search results alone, and never REJECT because of their absence.

The thresholds above are assumptions, not an approved company policy.
